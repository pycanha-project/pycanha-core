Detailed Description

An in-plane conductor between two adjacent cells of one item.

Cells are the linear indices of the ThermalMesh cell grid, k = i + j * (n1 - 1) with direction 1 varying fastest, and the link always joins the SAME side of both cells: side 1 and side 2 are two separate conducting sheets, each with its own thickness and bulk material. When both sides of a cell carry the same node number the two sheets end up as parallel conductors of that node pair, which is exactly the "a dual-surfaced node conducts through t1 + t2" rule.
