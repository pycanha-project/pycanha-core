Detailed Description

What the last solve() did. initialize() clears it.

The counters are aggregates kept during the run (a few additions per iteration and two clock reads around each callback); the text is built only by to_string(). Only the first max_listed_steps non-converged steps are listed, all of them are counted.

◆ reuse_error

double pycanha::solvers::SolverReport::reuse_error = 0.0

Estimated error the iteration on old factors leaves in the temperatures [K]: reuse_tolerance times the largest change of the last iteration of the steady state, or of any time step; 0 without reuse_factors. An order of magnitude, not a bound: LAPACK's FGMRES, which stops on the residual without the factors, and long transients, where it adds up over the steps, can leave a few times more. Rounding errors, the same as a direct solve's, come on top.
