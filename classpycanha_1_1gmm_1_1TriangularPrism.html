◆ to_uv()

Point2D pycanha::gmm::TriangularPrism::to_uv ( const Point3D & point) const
nodiscard

uv follows the Cube convention: the integer part of u selects one of the five faces – walls 0, 1 and 2 on the three base edges, then the base (3) and the top (4) – and its fraction is the first surface parameter, with v the second. The walls span their base edge and the extrusion; the two triangular ends use the strip parametrisation a Triangle does. Normals point out of the solid.
