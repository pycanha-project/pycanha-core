◆ fixed_couplings

bool pycanha::solvers::detail::NetworkOptions::fixed_couplings = false

The couplings and the diagonal passed to linearize() stay as they are at the first linearize() of each solve(): the user's promise, trusted and never checked.

◆ keep_matrix

bool pycanha::solvers::detail::NetworkOptions::keep_matrix = false

The matrix is still needed after solve() (multiply()); otherwise a dense factorization may overwrite it, and the next linearize() rebuilds it.

◆ skip_unchanged

bool pycanha::solvers::detail::NetworkOptions::skip_unchanged = false

solve() compares the matrix with the one last factorized and reuses the factors when they are equal. Not needed with fixed_couplings, which knows when the matrix changes.
