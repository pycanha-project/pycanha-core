Detailed Description

The settings of every solver type.

Each field is set (holds a value) or unset (the solver's default, which may depend on the solver type, the build, the other fields and the model). One Settings can be shared by solvers of different types: a set field that does not apply to a solver is ignored there with a warning (see resolve()). setting_fields() lists every field with its documentation.
