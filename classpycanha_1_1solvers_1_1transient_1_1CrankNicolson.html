Detailed Description

Crank-Nicolson transient with the radiation linearised at every inner iteration, solved with a sparse or dense direct factorization. The time and output settings (start_time, end_time, time_step, output_interval, output_attributes, output_name) are read by initialize(), which also creates the output model in the thermal data; time_step is required. The factorization is LU, or Cholesky for a model without radiative couplings (see Settings: factorization). Every inner iteration reads the couplings and the capacities again, so formulas and callbacks may change them during the run; with fixed_couplings they are read once per solve() and the matrix is factorized again only when the temperatures change it. With reuse_factors each inner iteration solves for the temperature change, so the residual of the iteration on old factors does not stay in the temperatures. A step whose inner iterations do not converge within max_iterations keeps its last iteration and the run goes on: the first such step logs a WARN, the end of the run one summary WARN, and report() lists them.
