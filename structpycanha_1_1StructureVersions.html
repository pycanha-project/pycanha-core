Detailed Description

Structure versions of the three parts of a network: the nodes (see Nodes::structure_version) and the conductive and radiative coupling patterns (see CouplingMatrices::structure_version). Two equal snapshots of one network mean the same nodes in the same order and the same stored couplings; values may differ.
