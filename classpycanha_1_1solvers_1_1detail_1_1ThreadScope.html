Detailed Description

Sets the threads of the calling thread for one MKL call and restores the previous setting afterwards. MKL's LAPACK follows MKL's setting, but the two-level PARDISO factorization follows OpenMP's, which defaults to every logical CPU: both are set. 0 keeps MKL's setting (MKL_NUM_THREADS, or the physical cores) and applies it to OpenMP too.
